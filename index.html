How I lead

Helping people grow.
Making the work better.

I build the teams, environments, and practices that help good design happen consistently. Clear direction. Honest critique. A close connection to customers.

Give people room to own the work.

I coach designers to make decisions, develop their judgment, and take meaningful ownership.

Make learning part of building.

I establish research and design practices that bring customer insight into everyday product decisions.

Keep the bar high, and stay involved.

I connect systems, guide key decisions, and get hands-on when it helps the team move the craft forward.

A little about me

Always an itch to build.

I get an itch to turn a messy problem into something useful. That’s taken me from invoicing with AI to the ways businesses pay and get paid.

That same builder’s instinct shapes how I lead: giving designers room to make decisions, using critique to sharpen the work, and making customer research part of how the team builds.
